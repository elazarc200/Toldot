# Methodology

1. Read the existing Toladot people/person-names/generation/chronology schema and Pirkei Avot pilot without changing them. Use UUID-shaped stable research IDs, names/aliases, directional teacher/student claims, source provenance, knowledge state and lifecycle status. This is an isolated data layer; no schema migration or pilot expansion.
2. Download full merged Hebrew base-text exports from the broad Chazal categories. Retain parallel editions, raw files, SHA-256 hashes and segment coordinates. Exclude modern commentaries and distinguish later compilations/reference introductions.
3. Collect identity candidates from both complete Sefaria topic branches, curated Bonayich/Sperling entity inventories and tagged mentions, Hebrew Wikipedia generation/region categories, Wikidata, and available Hyman print-page transcriptions. The 5,998-row Sefaria names inventory is a discovery resource, never a person count.
4. Normalize Hebrew pointing, punctuation and spacing for lookup. Do not equate רב and רבי, בן and בר, or numbered homonyms merely through normalization. Scan every downloaded segment with a token trie; preserve name-occurrence candidates separately.
5. Require a verified Hebrew occurrence at an independently attributed source passage: Sefaria entity annotation, or an exact primary citation in an identity reference. Lexical hits alone are insufficient. Store primary Chazal evidence separately from secondary identity references. Up to 40 initial evidence passages per candidate are retained with excerpts; merged records may have more. Full raw texts, upstream annotations and all lexical matches remain available in corpus-mentions.jsonl.
6. Merge explicit crosswalks and exact bibliographic transclusions. Additional cross-source merges require a source-attested name/compound alias, an independently attributed shared primary passage and compatible classification/chronology; retain the proof. A Hyman 'see entry' pointer is not identity equivalence: Avtalyon may point to Shemaiah without being the same person. Source-numbered homonyms remain separate.
7. Preserve different generation schemes. Resolve only evidence-backed Tanna/Amora classification; unknown stays null and outside the classified count. Geography comes from explicit category/identity assertions, not the spelling רב/רבי or the corpus in which someone appears. Region unknown is not Babylonia.
8. Preserve traditional reference text and its uncertainty. Modern disputes are not allowed to silently overwrite it. Claim-by-claim scholarly adjudication is not complete; raw citations and discussion are retained for review. Teacher/student values remain separate; quoted 'in the name of' is not automatically direct discipleship.
9. Count only records with primary evidence, a supported classification and no unresolved identity flags. High confidence requires corroborating retrieved identity sources; medium accepts narrower curated attribution. These are operational confidence levels, not a guarantee of historical certainty. Manual review contains candidate records and may contain several records for one person, or no sage at all.
10. Validate IDs, external identifiers, canonical duplicates, same-scheme generation contradictions, Tanna/Amora conflicts, available dates, source text coordinates and source-name occurrence. Missing dates/relationships are reported as untestable, not valid.

The requested universal historical census is not certified complete: the resolved count is conditional on these rules and the processed corpus, with remaining identity work explicitly retained.
