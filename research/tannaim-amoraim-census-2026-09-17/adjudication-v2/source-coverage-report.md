Identity evidence: 1210 Sefaria topic requests (2 empty/dangling graph targets), 744 Hebrew Wikipedia pages and 737 Wikidata entities. Wikipedia category traversal includes non-sages; category membership alone does not admit a person.

Hyman: 2273 subarticle/index pages; 777 available transcribed print pages; 2349 parsed sections. 45 referenced print-page titles had no available revision, and 110 pages were not section-segmented (including introductions and continuations). Full print-page text is retained.

- Corpus scan is exhaustive over the selected downloaded files, not a claim that every relevant manuscript/version or all Sefaria categories were included.
- Merged Hebrew editions are not a manuscript-variant census. Parallel Tosefta editions are retained, not counted as extra people.
- Hyman Wikisource subarticles are incomplete. All available print-page transcriptions were enumerated; absent and unsegmented pages are disclosed.
- Some print-page transcriptions are not proofread.
- Primary textual occurrence plus an identity attribution is required for counted records; unanchored lexical matches are excluded.
- Sefaria NER, Wikipedia and reference-work interpretations may still contain errors.
- Traditional-versus-modern disputes are retained in original reference text; exhaustive claim-level adjudication remains incomplete.
